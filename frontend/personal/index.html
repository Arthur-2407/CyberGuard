WHAT WE LOOK FOR

Clues that a link may not be safe.

  • Known malicious or phishing reports
  • Suspicious destination and domain signals
  • Redirects that lead somewhere unexpected

A clean result is not a guarantee. New or targeted threats may not yet be known to security services.
